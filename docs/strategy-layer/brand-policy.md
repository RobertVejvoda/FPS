# FairSpot Brand Policy

FairSpot is open-source software under AGPL-3.0-or-later. The software license lets people copy, modify, run, and redistribute the code under the license terms. This brand policy covers the FairSpot name, logo, and project identity.

## Brand Ownership

The FairSpot name, logo, and project identity are brand assets associated with Robert Vejvoda's FairSpot project.

Using the AGPL-licensed source code does not grant permission to present a modified product, hosted service, commercial offer, or fork as official FairSpot unless Robert Vejvoda separately agrees in writing.

## Allowed Use

You may use the FairSpot name to:

- refer truthfully to the original upstream project;
- state that your work is based on FairSpot;
- link to the upstream repository;
- preserve copyright, license, source, and attribution notices required by the project license.

## Restricted Use

Do not use the FairSpot name or logo in a way that implies:

- your fork, hosted service, support package, or commercial offer is official FairSpot;
- Robert Vejvoda endorses, certifies, supports, or operates your deployment;
- your modified version is the same product as the upstream project when users could reasonably be confused.

Modified forks and commercial deployments should use their own product or company branding and include a clear notice such as "based on FairSpot" with a link to the upstream source.

## In-Product Legal Notice

FairSpot distributions should keep a visible legal or about screen that includes:

- copyright notice: `Copyright (c) 2026 Robert Vejvoda`;
- license: `AGPL-3.0-or-later`;
- upstream source link: `https://github.com/RobertVejvoda/fairspot`;
- a note that the FairSpot name and logo identify Robert Vejvoda's project.

## No Warranty Or Support Promise

Brand attribution does not create a support, warranty, hosting, certification, or commercial relationship. Separate written terms are required for paid support, dual licensing, certification, or official hosted operation.
