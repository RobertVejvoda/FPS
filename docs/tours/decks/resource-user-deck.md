# Resource User Deck

A slide outline for an end-user "how it works" intro. One slide per section; each carries a **Source:** link. Presenter view of the [Resource User Tour](../resource-user).

---

## 1 — Getting a space, fairly

- You need a scarce space (parking today; seats, courts, desks later).
- FairSpot gives everyone a fair chance and always tells you your status and next action.

*Source: [Resource User Tour](../resource-user)*

---

## 2 — Sign in

- Sign in with your organisation identity (SSO) or a FairSpot account.
- You never type a tenant or user id — it's resolved from your sign-in.

*Source: [Tenant Discovery and Login Modes](../../business-layer/tenant-login-modes)*

---

## 3 — Request capacity

- Ask for a space for a date and location.
- Your request appears immediately with a safe status and, where relevant, an employee-visible reason.

*Source: [Resource User Tour → The journey](../resource-user)*

---

## 4 — The Draw decides fairly

- When demand &gt; supply, documented fairness rules allocate the spaces.
- Obligations (company car) are honoured first; everyone else is weighted by recent history — miss out once, and your chances improve next time.
- You see your result, not the lottery internals.

*Source: [Product Overview → Current Product Shape](../../Home#current-product-shape)*

---

## 5 — Get notified &amp; see My Bookings

- A notification records the outcome (allocated, waitlisted, or a safe reason).
- My Bookings shows what you requested and its state.

*Source: [Resource User Tour → The journey](../resource-user)*

---

## 6 — Cancel keeps it fair

- Cancel a space you don't need and the next fairly-ranked waitlisted person is promoted.
- A freed space still goes to someone by the rules.

*Source: [Resource User Tour → The journey](../resource-user)*

---

## 7 — What you'll never see

- Your own bookings, your own notifications, understandable reasons — **not** other users, not hidden allocation internals.
- A product guarantee, not a UI accident.

*Source: [Client Evaluation Pack → Security &amp; GDPR](../../client-evaluation-pack#security-and-gdpr-summary)*
