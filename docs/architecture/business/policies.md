# Business Policies

| Policy | Rule | Owner | Source Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Company-car priority | Company-car employees are allocated first where policy requires it. | Product / Tenant policy owner | [Allocation Rules](/business-layer/allocation-rules) |
| Weighted fairness | Non-company-car allocation uses recent allocation and active penalty facts. | Product / Tenant policy owner | [Allocation Rules](/business-layer/allocation-rules) |
| Same-day support | Employees can request same-day parking outside the scheduled Draw path. | Product owner | [Booking](/business-layer/booking) |
| Late cancellation | Late cancellation starts after allocation in v1. | Product owner | [Booking Request Lifecycle](/business-layer/booking-request-lifecycle) |
| Mandatory operational notifications | Critical booking/allocation/cancellation events cannot be disabled by user preference. | Product / Compliance | [Notification](/business-layer/notification) |
| Billing deferred | Billing and subscription enforcement are not part of the customer-ready target. | Product sponsor | [Commercialisation](/strategy-layer/commercialisation) |
