# Value Streams

| Value Stream | Trigger | Outcome | Key Capabilities |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tenant goes live | Company wants to pilot or run FairSpot. | Tenant is configured, identity is mapped, policy/locations exist, and readiness is visible. | Tenant onboarding, configuration, identity, readiness. |
| Employee gets parking outcome | Employee needs parking. | Employee submits a request and receives an understandable allocation, rejection, or pending outcome. | Employee request, fair allocation, notification, My Spots. |
| HR manages exceptions | Operational issue, cancellation, or support request occurs. | HR/facility manager can inspect, cancel, trigger controlled Draw action, and notify affected employees. | HR operations, audit, notification. |
| Auditor reviews fairness | Dispute, compliance review, or management question occurs. | Auditor can review business evidence without exposing unnecessary personal data. | Audit, reporting, privacy controls. |
| Customer evaluates deployment | Client IT or sponsor reviews product readiness. | Deployment profile, WAF/auth/secret/backup/observability expectations are clear. | Production, security, technology, readiness. |
